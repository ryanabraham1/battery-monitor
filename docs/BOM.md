## 5. What it adds up to

Per node, parts only (board parts + hand-solder parts + off-board parts above, not counting what you
already have): **about $21–29**. On top of that the JLCPCB order carries fixed costs that are shared
across the batch: bare boards, assembly setup, the extended-part fees and shipping, roughly $100–160 for
ten boards. The hub is about $45–60 with a Pi Zero 2 W.
